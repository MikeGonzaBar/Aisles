3 — In context

Aisles
WALK THE AISLES

What to check: at 16 through 48 you should still read “a blue case on a yellow shelf” — that's the flat vector mark doing its job. At 64 and up the rich mark takes over and the shelf fills out into three objects. The handoff sits at 64 because that's where the rich mark stops holding: measured on a scanline through the cases it keeps 39 distinct colour bands at 64, drops to 33 by 48, and its rental yellow drifts from #FFDE00 toward #574400 by 16.

Two sources, one file: 16/24/32/48 are rendered from prototype/icon-concept-a-small.svg; 64/128/256 are downscaled from prototype/icon-rich-source.png. Move the handoff by editing the PLAN array at the top of tools/build-icon.js.

Structure: 7 entries — 6 uncompressed 32-bit DIBs (16→128) plus one PNG-compressed 256, which is the convention Windows expects. Alpha is carried per-pixel, so the rounded corners are genuinely transparent, not white.

Rebuild: node tools/build-icon.js — swap a source or move the handoff, re-run, done.
