Bands counts runs of distinct colour along one row, so it over-counts anti-aliasing — read the trend, not the number. What matters is the palette at the bottom: at 16px the mark's rental yellow #FFDE00 has become #574400 and the bright blue #0052BC has become #4C6C96. The colours have turned to mud, which is exactly what a vector doesn't do.

My read: the rich mark is genuinely good from 64px up — it's the best-looking version of this logo so far, and it should be the 256/128/64 entries. Below 64 the flat vector wins outright, because it's drawn at the size it's displayed rather than shrunk toward it. That's the tiered structure, confirmed by measurement rather than taste.
