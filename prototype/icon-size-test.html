Reading it: the full mark holds down to about 48px, then the two spines blur together and the case loses its edge. The small mark stays legible to 16px because there's one shape and one idea.

The rule to ship with: 256/128/64/48/32 → full. 24/16 → small. Windows will pick the right one from a multi-size .ico automatically, so build both into one file.

Still flat-colour vectors — no gradients, no outlines that vanish when scaled, nothing that needs anti-aliasing to survive. That's why they hold up.
